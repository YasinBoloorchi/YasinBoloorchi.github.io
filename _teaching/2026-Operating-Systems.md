I served as a Teaching Assistant for the Operating Systems course during Spring 2026 at the University of Iowa. The course covered core operating system concepts such as process scheduling, virtual memory, synchronization, inter-process communication, file systems.

My responsibilities included conducting office hours, assisting students with low-level programming assignments, grading homework. This experience gave me a deeper appreciation for the complexity of modern operating systems and further strengthened my background in systems programming and concurrent software design.
