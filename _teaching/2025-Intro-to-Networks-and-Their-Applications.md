During Fall 2025, I worked as a Teaching Assistant for the Introduction to Networks and Their Applications course. This course explored the fundamentals of computer networking, including Internet architecture, socket programming, routing, TCP/UDP, and application-layer protocols.

My responsibilities included holding office hours, helping students with programming assignments and networking concepts, grading homework, and supporting students throughout the semester. Answering students questions on network applications and communication protocols made this one of the most enjoyable teaching experiences I have had, especially given how closely the material aligns with my research interests.
